## Description

> **Content warning:** blood, death, religious imagery, flashing images.

**«Here am I.»** Isaiah said it when he saw the seraphim.

You wake from a coma in the Threshold Hospital. The world has fifteen minutes left. The nurses still change the sheets.
A doctor counts to forty-one with his forehead against the wall. A man in the corner begs you not to look at the
ceiling.

Look at the ceiling.

**HINENI** is a short first-person horror game rendered entirely in ASCII: a real 3D hospital turned into characters
by shape, like an engraving that moves. There is no combat. Reading and choosing stop the clock; walking does not.

- **Fifteen minutes, six endings.** The last thing you do decides how it ends. Every ending has its own look, and you
  can revisit the ones you have found from the menu.
- **What is above the ceiling.** Wheels full of eyes and bodies with four faces, praying, weeping blood, speaking in
  tongues. They pick up where they left off when you look back up.
- **Five languages, chosen separately for text and voice:** English, Spanish, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese and
  Japanese. Every line is voiced.
- **Plays in the browser, on desktop and on phones** (landscape, two touch sticks).

**Controls:** WASD walk · mouse look · E interact · F flashlight · ESC pause · F5 save. On phones: two sticks and the
Controls button.

**macOS download:** the app is not signed with an Apple certificate. The first time, right-click Hineni → Open → Open.

Made by Jorge Polanco. Source code (MIT) and full credits: https://github.com/Jorge-Polanco-Roque/hineni
