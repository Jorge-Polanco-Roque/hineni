<h3>NOW</h3>
<p><strong><a href="https://roqueio.itch.io/hineni">HINENI</a></strong> — the last fifteen minutes before the Judgment, in a hospital that kept working. A first-person horror game rendered entirely in ASCII. Six endings, five languages, every line voiced. Playable in the browser, on Windows, macOS and Linux.</p>

<h3>WHAT I'M LEARNING BY BUILDING</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Engines &amp; code:</strong> Unity and C#, gameplay systems, state and save data, cross-platform builds (desktop, WebGL, mobile).</li>
  <li><strong>Rendering:</strong> custom shaders and post-processing, an ASCII renderer that matches characters by shape, procedural skies and effects.</li>
  <li><strong>Worlds:</strong> procedural level construction, character rigging and inverse kinematics, lighting for atmosphere.</li>
  <li><strong>Sound &amp; voice:</strong> dialogue pipelines with text-to-speech, spatial audio, adaptive ambience.</li>
  <li><strong>Shipping:</strong> localization, automated tests and screenshot pipelines, publishing and updates.</li>
</ul>
